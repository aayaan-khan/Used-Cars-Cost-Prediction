# Decision log

- D1: Primary dataset is CarDekho Car details v3 (Indian market, has owner and seller_type).
- D2: UK audi.csv is only an optional secondary generalisation check, never mixed into training.
- D3: De-duplicate before any train/test split (1,202 exact duplicate rows found).
- D4: "torque" is a candidate to drop (inconsistent units).
- D5: units parsed to numeric; "torque" dropped (inconsistent formats).
- D6: age = 2020 - year, because the data ends in 2020.
- D7: custom model_key (218 groups) replaces the naive two-word key.
- D8: outlier rule is implied km per year > 100,000 (4 rows removed); high-priced cars are kept.
- D9: 87 CNG/LPG rows report mileage in km/kg, so fuel stays in the model.
- D10: the 209 rows with missing specs are informative (older, cheaper); no dropna. Impute within model and fuel inside CV folds, plus a missing flag.
- D11: target is log(price): skew 5.6 -> -0.16, and depreciation is multiplicative.
- D12: raw brand and mileage comparisons are confounded; report within-model estimates (age -9.8%/yr, 10k km -0.9%, diesel +13.6%, automatic +11.3%, non-first owner -7.7%).
- D13: final test set is 20% of car models, locked until the last step.
- D14: development CV is StratifiedGroupKFold (groups = model, stratified by price band).
- D15: select models on grouped CV, and also report the random-split (seen-model) number.
- D16: baselines B0 median, B1 median by age, B2 age + log km; bar to clear is MedAPE about 29%.
- D17: leakage demo: R2 0.934 (duplicates, random) -> 0.906 (de-duplicated, random) -> 0.810 (grouped by model).
- D18: features exclude model identity; model_key is only the grouping key for validation.
- D19: all preprocessing (median imputation with missing-indicators, scaling, one-hot) sits inside the pipeline.
- D20: every model is scored in two regimes: grouped by model (stress test) and random on de-duplicated data (typical repeat listing).
- D21: model-aware imputation tested and rejected (no consistent gain); this revises D10 to plain median imputation plus indicators.
- D22: selection rule = one-standard-deviation band on grouped RMSE_log, then best typical-listing MedAPE -> LightGBM (15 leaves, 500 trees, lr 0.03). Final test set untouched.
- D23: prediction intervals use Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) at nominal 90% (alpha=0.10, 5th to 95th percentiles) on log(price) to guarantee distribution-free finite-sample coverage.
- D24: base quantiles use LightGBM quantile regression (15 leaves, 500 trees, lr 0.03) vs linear QuantileRegressor baseline; linear quantiles over-inflate to 222% width, while LightGBM with CQR delivers competitive 64-87% widths and lower Winkler score.
- D25: calibration sets are held out strictly by model_key inside training folds (nested 4:1 split) without touching the locked test set.
- D26: slice diagnostics on unseen models under standard LightGBM_CQR measure 80.8% coverage in true Q1 and 83.5% in true Q5 (vs 93%+ in middle tiers); when sliced by predicted price quintile, coverage is 88.2% (P1), 87.2% (P2), 83.5% (P3), 92.0% (P4), and 90.5% (P5).
- D27: Mondrian bins are constructed on predicted log-mid-price from the LightGBM quantile base models on the calibration fold; no true price labels are used at fit or inference time, making this variant fully deployable without ground-truth leakage.
- D28: LightGBM_CQR 5-fold stability measures mean coverage 88.27% +/- 5.13% (grouped) and 89.53% +/- 1.42% (seen); median width measures 86.30% +/- 5.96% (grouped) and 63.98% +/- 2.75% (seen).
- D29: calibration split sensitivity across 5 random seeds (0, 7, 21, 42, 99) measures global Q_adj range of 0.0169 (min 0.0877, max 0.1046, default seed 42 = 0.0911).
- D30: fuel slice breakdown by age and predicted price measures lower coverage for diesel in older buckets (80.7% in 8-12 yrs, 79.1% in 13+ yrs vs petrol 91.5% and 87.2%) and lower predicted price quintiles (78.9% in P1 vs petrol 90.5%); reported without causal claims.
- D31: Section 6c evaluated three candidates to address diesel under-coverage (A: LightGBM_CQR, B: Fuel-conditional CQR with pooled CNG/LPG, C: Locally Adaptive CQR normalised by predicted difficulty sigma(x)). Programmatic decision rule evaluated worst-segment coverage (Diesel/Petrol x predicted-price quintile) subject to grouped coverage >= 88% and median width <= 1.10 x Candidate A (<= 95.59%). Candidate C achieved worst-segment coverage 79.1% (P3 Diesel) vs 78.9% for Candidates A (P1 Diesel) and B (P3 Diesel), with overall grouped coverage 88.17% (seen 89.71%) and median width 85.7% (seen 64.8%), winning selection as C_Adaptive_CQR. Seed stability across 5 inner calibration seeds in full outer 5-fold CV measured out-of-fold grouped coverage range of 1.05% (88.17% to 89.22%), median width range of 2.0% (85.7% to 87.7%), and mean calibration adjustment range of 0.010 (0.157 to 0.167). Candidate C artifact saved to models/c_adaptive_cqr_model.joblib.
- D32: the pre-committed rule had no minimum-improvement margin, so a 0.2-point difference decided the selection; any future pre-registered rule should include one. No candidate closed the diesel coverage gap.
- D33: Section 6 is frozen; downstream sections use candidate C intervals with the diesel under-coverage disclosed.
- D35: the CQR classes (MondrianCQR, FuelMondrianCQR, LocallyAdaptiveCQR) and compute_cqr_adjustment moved from the notebook to src/conformal.py, because a class defined in a notebook is pickled as __main__.<Class> and cannot be loaded elsewhere.
